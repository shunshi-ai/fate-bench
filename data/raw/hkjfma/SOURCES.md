# Sources — HKJFMA Global Fortune Teller Competition

## The competition

The **Global Fortune Teller Competition** (全球算命師大賽 / 全球算命師比賽) has been organized
annually since 2010 by the **Hong Kong Junior Fengshui Master Association**
(香港青年術數家協會, [hkjfma.org](https://hkjfma.org)).

Each edition works the same way. A panel of practising masters each contributes one real case:
a birth date, hour, place, and gender, together with a set of multiple-choice questions about that
person's documented history — the year they married, the year a parent died, the year a business
was founded. Entrants submit answers by a deadline. After the contest closes, the association
publishes the answer key and the winners.

Scoring, from the 5th edition onward, drops each entrant's best-scoring case before totalling —
the organizers describe this as modelled on competitive diving.

## What is collected here

Only material that the organizer published openly on its own public website. No entrant
submissions, no member-only material, no personal identifiers beyond what the organizer printed
(birth data and gender; the subjects are not named).

`robots.txt` at hkjfma.org disallows only `/wp-admin/`; the pages collected here are permitted to
crawlers.

Per-page provenance — the exact source URL for every file — is recorded in the header of each
`.txt` file and in `_pages_index.json`.

## Attribution

Individual cases carry the name of the master who contributed them (命題提供者). Where the source
records that name, it is preserved in the extracted text, and it should be preserved in any
normalized form derived from it.

## Prior work

The 13th–16th editions (2022–2025) were previously transcribed and normalized by
[**MingLi-Bench**](https://github.com/DestinyLinker/MingLi-Bench) (Destiny Linker), released under
the MIT License. That work is reused here with attribution. This repository extends coverage
backwards to the earlier editions, which had not previously been collected.
